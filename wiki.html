Alphonse Summers

From AU Wiki, the fictional encyclopedia · This page concerns the story character.

Alphonse “Alfie” Benjamin Summers is a Canadian-American teenage actor and student in the Alphonse Summers AU. He attends Starlight Performing Arts High School in New York, where he is a sophomore. His life combines school and acting work with an emerging mystery involving a ring he initially mistook for a costume prop.

Profile

Alfie is portrayed as grounded, kind, witty, nerdy, anxious at times, and emotionally honest. Though he is a young performer with public recognition, he remains a student managing homework, rehearsal, set schedules, and ordinary family life. He often uses humor to ease awkward moments, but takes other people's safety seriously.

Background and family

Alfie is Canadian-American and lives in New York while attending Starlight. His parents are Joanne and Dakota Summers. He has six siblings: Tobias, Alistair, Valerie, twins Keira and Elias, and Liliana. He misses home and stays in touch with his family, whose group chat is a recurring part of his everyday life.

Acting and school

Alfie works as an actor while studying at Starlight Performing Arts High School. He balances classes and assignments with rehearsals and production work. He values the contributions of cast, crew, costume, and other staff, and tries to thank them. Specific production titles, awards, and dates have not been established in the AU.

The ring and Stellaris

After a wrap party, Alfie found a ring and assumed it was a prop. The ring glowed after he touched it, and unusual jellyfish-like entities appeared. The events connect to Stellaris and an archivist who knows more about the phenomenon. Alfie is still learning what the ring does and what is happening; the limits and origin of its abilities remain unclear. He is cautious about experimenting with it around other people.

Personal life and interests

Charlotte is Alfie's girlfriend and a source of support; she notices when he is anxious or pretending to be fine. Alfie plays guitar and piano, sings, and writes songs and lyrics. He likes fruit mochi, apple pie, soufflé pancakes, poutine, donuts, and spicy food; he is allergic to seafood.

His animals include Anne-Marie, a Persian cat; Dex, a chihuahua; Joyce, a teacup Pomeranian; and Mayfield, his horse. Anne-Marie is known for sitting on his scripts, while Dex and Joyce bring their own brand of household chaos.

Story status

The ring mystery and its connection to Stellaris are ongoing. Further details should be added as they are established in the fictional story, rather than treated as real-world facts.
